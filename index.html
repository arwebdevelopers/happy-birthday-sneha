A Letter, Just for You

Some things are easier to write than say.

Dear Twinkle Di ✨

Happy Birthday to my forever big sister, my safe place, and the family I got to choose.

When I look back at my favorite chapters, almost all of them have your face in them. I still smile whenever I think of the times we laughed until our stomachs hurt over nothing at all. You didn't just walk through life alongside me; you made the ordinary days feel like adventures and the heavy days feel a little bit lighter.

Growing up—or rather, growing older—with you has been one of the greatest gifts I never knew I needed. Even though we don't share the same childhood home or the same mom, you've always known how to calm my storms, celebrate my small wins, and tell me the hard truths when I needed to hear them most. You’ve been my big sister in every single way that truly counts.

Thank you for every shared secret, every comfort, and every single memory we’ve built so far. I can’t wait to see what kind of laughs, challenges, and milestones we get to collect together in the year ahead.

May your day be filled with as much warmth, love, and light as you bring into my life every single day.

With all my love,
Your Puchku ⭐
